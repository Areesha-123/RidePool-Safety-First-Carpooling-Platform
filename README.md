Co-built and pitched a safety-first carpooling platform designed for students and professionals in Lahore, addressing urban commute costs and women's transport safety. Designed and prototyped core features including verified ride matching, a Women-Only Mode, live route tracking (OpenStreetMap/OSRM), an automated fare-split calculator, and a full SOS emergency flow with live location sharing and safety team alerts. Delivered a complete business model and financial plan alongside the interactive prototype. Awarded Top Project Recognition at Riphah International University, 2026.
